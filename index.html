The Real Product

Ten mental models, not twelve fund picks

This is what you actually take away - frameworks that keep working long after the engagement ends.

01

Boring Is Beautiful

Wealth is built by unremarkable actions repeated without interruption. Excitement in a portfolio is a cost, not a feature.

02

Rate-Ranked Debt Attack

Debt isn't a feeling, it's a spreadsheet sorted by interest rate. Expensive debt dies first; cheap debt can be kept deliberately.

03

Simultaneity Over Sequence

Conventional advice says clear debt, then invest. That wastes years of compounding. Both engines run at once, in the right ratio.

04

Debt-Free Day Is a Redeployment Event

The month the last loan closes, every rupee that was servicing it needs a named destination - or lifestyle inflation eats it silently.

05

Structural Gaps Beat Return Chasing

An unregistered Will is a bigger risk than 2% of underperformance. Fix the plumbing before optimising the flow.

06

The Invest-to-Spend Ratio

The one number that predicts wealth: money into assets versus money into lifestyle. Most high earners are inverted and can't say why.

07

Illiquidity Honesty

Net worth on paper means little if most of it is land, house and gold. Naming that out loud is more useful than celebrating the headline figure.

08

Written Exit Levels

No position gets averaged down without a pre-written exit level. This converts emotional holding into a documented decision.

09

Insurance Is Not Investment

A large term cover for a fraction of what one endowment policy costs. Cover the catastrophe, invest the rest. Never blend the two.

10

Design for Your Absence

Registered Will, named executor, named guardian, correct nominations, a spouse who knows where everything is. A plan that only works while you're alive isn't a plan.

Fit Check

Who this is for - and who it isn't

This is for you if

  • You're ready to build a real system for your money, whatever stage you're starting from
  • You earn well but cannot explain where the money goes
  • You're early in your career and want to build the right habits now, not fix them later
  • Six SIPs, three policies, two loans, no strategy connecting them
  • You're the first wealth-builder in your family, with no one to ask
  • You want a partner who will disagree with you

This isn't for you if

  • You want stock tips or the next multibagger
  • You want someone to validate a decision you've already made
  • You want a quick fix, hot sector, or a guaranteed number
  • You're unwilling to write things down and review them
  • You want a product recommendation, not a system
  • You're looking for a SEBI-registered advisor to place trades
